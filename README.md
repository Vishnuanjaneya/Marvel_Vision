# 🦸‍♂️ MARVEL VISION

### Real-Time Superhero Augmented Reality powered by Computer Vision

> **Turn your webcam into a superhero interface.**
>
> MARVEL VISION is a real-time computer vision experience that tracks your hands and body through a webcam and transforms natural gestures into cinematic superhero powers — rendered directly onto the live camera feed.

## ⚡ The Experience

MARVEL VISION combines **real-time computer vision, gesture recognition, body segmentation and GPU/CPU-friendly canvas rendering** to create an interactive superhero experience.

There are no uploaded images.

There are no pre-rendered superhero videos.

There are no static overlays.

Everything happens **live**.
